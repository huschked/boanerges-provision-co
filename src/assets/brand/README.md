# Boanerges Brand Marks

## Primary wordmark

Use `../BoanergesLogoTransparent-v2.png` in wide layouts such as the desktop website header and transactional email.

## Detailed crest

- `boanerges-crest-detailed.png`: full-color packaging and marketing crest.
- `boanerges-crest-gold.png`: gold-only mark for dark backgrounds and specialty printing.
- `boanerges-crest-black.png`: black-only mark for light backgrounds and single-ink printing.

Avoid using the detailed crest below 96 px because the laurel and star details will close up.

## Simplified monogram

- `boanerges-monogram.png`: two-color favicon, mobile-header, avatar, and small product seal.
- `boanerges-monogram-gold.png`: gold-only small mark.
- `boanerges-monogram-black.png`: black-only small mark.
- `boanerges-monogram.svg`: editable vector-friendly source for the simplified mark.

Use the simplified monogram below 96 px. The SVG keeps the `B` as editable Georgia text; convert the text to outlines before sending it to a print vendor.

## Stacked lockup

- `boanerges-stacked-lockup.png`: full-color formal lockup.
- `boanerges-stacked-lockup-gold.png`: gold-only lockup for dark backgrounds.
- `boanerges-stacked-lockup-black.png`: black-only lockup for light backgrounds.

Use stacked lockups in vertical or square spaces such as the footer, packaging panels, cards, and marketing compositions. Continue using the horizontal wordmark where vertical space is constrained.

## Color

- Brand gold: `#C5A059`
- Brand black: `#0B0B0B`

Keep clear space around every mark equal to at least the width of the lightning bolt. Do not stretch, rotate, recolor, add effects, or combine the crest and horizontal wordmark in a different arrangement.
